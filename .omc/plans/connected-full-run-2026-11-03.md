## Throughput change

An LRU cache retains at most four decoded bundles in total, counting both idle
cache entries and exclusive worker leases. Dirty entries are authoritative in
memory; updates advance logical version/revision/optimizer metadata immediately.
Eviction writes, hashes and syncs the complete bundle before dropping it.
Checkpoint publication flushes dirty entries first. Evaluation flushes before
its immutable-state fingerprint. A failed writeback poisons the store rather
than falling back to stale durable state.

Lease fields become private; immutable getters and an explicit mutable accessor
prevent unchecked writes during read-only operations. Shared-memory publication
order, random route draws, update arithmetic and persisted numerical identity
remain unchanged. Memory limits include transient loads and cached optimizers.
